Questions

The short answers.

Is DiplomaMate really free?

Yes — free for students. There are no fees inside the app.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 9 and newer. On iPhone and computer, use the web app at diplomamate.online — it works in the browser and can be added to the home screen.

Do I need an account?

Yes — register once with your Board Roll and Registration Number, then set a PIN. Your account, routine and community stay saved, on the app and the website.

Where do the results come from?

From BTEB Result Zone and the Official BTEB portal. DiplomaMate shows them clearly and credits the source — it does not publish its own results.

Is my data safe?

Your data lives in a secure database, and you can delete your account anytime from Settings in the app, or from the delete-account page linked below.
